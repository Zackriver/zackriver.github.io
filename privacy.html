Privacy Policy

BRICK BREAKER FOR LEGEND'S · Developer: Zahir Dev · Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game BRICK BREAKER FOR LEGEND'S ("the game") collects, why, and what you can do about it. The game is developed by Zahir Dev ("we", "us"). If you have any question, write to zahirdev82@gmail.com.

1. Summary

  • We do not ask for your real name, address, phone number or email inside the game.
  • The game uses services from Unity and Google to run online features, show ads, handle purchases, and find crashes.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The game is meant for players aged 13 and older.

3. Advertising

The game shows ads through Unity LevelPlay, which works with other ad networks. These companies may use your device's advertising ID and device information to show and measure ads, and may show personalised ads where the law allows it. You can reset your advertising ID or turn off ad personalisation in your phone's settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads). Buying any item in the shop removes the full-screen ads between levels.

4. Third-party services

These services have their own privacy policies:

  • Unity (Gaming Services, Analytics, Diagnostics, LevelPlay, IAP): unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google Play and Google Play Games: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, write to us and we will delete it.

6. How long we keep information

Game progress stays on your device until you uninstall the game or use Reset Progress in Settings. Online data (player ID, player name, clubs, rankings) is kept while you play and deleted on request. Analytics and crash data are kept by Unity for a limited time under Unity's policy.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your data: write to zahirdev82@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". Include your player name if you have one. We will delete your online data within 30 days.
  • Local data: Settings > Reset Progress, or uninstall the game.
  • Ads: see section 3.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK, Brazil or California), you may also have the right to see, correct or receive a copy of your data, or to object to its use. Write to us to use these rights.

8. Security

Data sent to online services travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but we use trusted providers and collect only what the game needs.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Important changes will also be announced in the game.
